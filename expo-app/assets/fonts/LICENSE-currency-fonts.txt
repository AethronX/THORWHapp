CurrencyOMR-Regular.ttf — Omani rial sign (U+20C4)
The glyph was redrawn by the Tharwati project as clean geometry (two slanted bars, hook and tail)
from the Central Bank of Oman's published symbol image supplied by the app owner. The sign itself is
the official currency symbol of the Sultanate of Oman. Source geometry and build script:
expo-app/scripts/omr-sign/ (fontTools + skia-pathops). Replace with the official vector when CBO
publishes one.
